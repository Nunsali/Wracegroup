About Wrace

Wrace Group is a privately-owned Nigerian real estate development company shaping communities across the city, one property at a time.

Wrace Group builds the places Abuja lives, shops, and grows in. From our flagship mall in Wuse 2, to a growing portfolio of residential estates and student hostels, every development is built on one principle: strategy first, execution always.
