About Wrace

Wrace Group is a privately-owned Nigerian real estate development company building residential and commercial assets across Abuja. Its portfolio includes The District by Wrace — a retail and lifestyle mall in Wuse 2 — a growing collection of residential estates, and student hostel accommodation through Wrace Hall and Wrace Court.
